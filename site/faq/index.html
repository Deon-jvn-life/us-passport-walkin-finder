Passport FAQ for South Florida

Can I walk in at a post office?

USPS listings in the locator point to usps.com/scheduler for appointments and additional times [source, source]. Some USPS hours in the data are flagged walk-in. See the walk-in page.

Which South Florida offices say they take walk-ins?

Broward Clerk of Courts lists passport hours at its Central, North, South and West courthouses [source], and the locator comments for its regional courthouses say appointments are not required [source]. Palm Beach Clerk says walk-in customers are not accepted [source]. Miami-Dade Public Library says its passport service is by appointment only [source].

How much does a first adult passport book cost?

$130 to the U.S. Department of State plus $35 to the acceptance facility [source].

Can I renew at an acceptance facility?

If you are eligible for DS-82, you renew by mail or online. Postal employees should not charge the $35 fee or review a DS-82 [source].

I travel in less than two weeks. Where do I go?

Make an appointment at a passport agency within 14 days of travel. The nearest is the Miami Passport Agency; call 877-487-2778 [source, source].

Do I need both parents for a child's passport?

For a child under 16, both parents or guardians must be present or give approval, for example with a notarized DS-3053. Sole custody uses different documents, and ages 16 and 17 follow another rule. Child under 16. [source, source]

Can I bring a digital birth certificate?

No. Physical evidence of citizenship only [source].

Do facilities take photos?

Some do; the locator flags 'photos on site' [source]. Palm Beach Clerk sells photos for $16 each [source]. Miami-Dade libraries say photo service is not currently available [source].
