Passport acceptance facilities in Miami-Dade County

30 facilities in the official locator, 5 with at least some hours flagged walk-in. County is assigned from the facility ZIP code using the Census 2020 ZCTA to county file [source].

Call or check online before you go. The locator says to check with the facility whether you need an appointment [source]. USPS locations direct you to usps.com/scheduler [source]. Hours below are as published in the official locator data on the date shown.
Miami-Dade Public Library System passport services

North Dade Regional, West Kendall Regional and Naranja Branch libraries accept passport applications by appointment only; one appointment per applicant. Photo service is currently not available. The library's own page lists North Dade Regional Monday hours as 4:30 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. [source]

City of Coral Gables passport office

Applications are by appointment only. The office is closed Wednesday, 11 November 2026, for Veterans Day, and reopens Thursday, 12 November 2026. [source]

City of Miami Beach passport hours

Passport customers are seen Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., by appointment only. [source]

City of Miami Springs passport services

Monday to Friday 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. and Saturday 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Appointments are prioritized, and a walk-in may not be served the same day. [source]

City of West Miami passport services

Monday to Friday 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., last appointment 3:30 p.m. Tuesday 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., last appointment 5:30 p.m. Appointments are online only. Walk-ins are accepted if a slot is open. [source]
