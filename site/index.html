Who needs to apply in person

  • First-time adult applicants, and adults whose passport was lost, stolen or damaged, issued over 15 years ago, or issued when they were under 16, must apply in person with Form DS-11 [source].
  • All children under 16 must apply in person, usually with both parents or guardians [source]. What to bring for a child under 16.
  • Eligible adults can renew by mail (DS-82) or online, and postal employees should not charge the $35 facility fee for a DS-82 renewal [source].
Miami Passport Agency (urgent travel only)

Omni Center, 1501 Biscayne Blvd, Suite 400, Miami, FL 33132. Get directions to the Miami Passport Agency, opens in a new tab

Monday to Friday 7:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., closed weekends and federal holidays. By appointment only. Miami is in a State Department pilot with the Detroit and San Juan passport agencies: you can make an appointment for urgent international travel within 21 calendar days, or if you need a foreign visa within 28 calendar days. Appointments: 877-487-2778 [source]. This is not an acceptance facility; routine applications go to the places listed on this site. What to bring

  • Fees and documentsAll amounts as stated by travel.state.gov on 26 October 2026. This page covers which form to use, the fees, how to pay, the documents to bring, the photo rules and the processing times.
  • Urgent travelThe Miami Passport Agency takes urgent-travel appointments. Its page allows booking within 21 calendar days of travel, or 28 if you need a foreign visa. Other State Department pages still say 14 days. Bring printed proof of travel. You cannot walk in.
  • Child under 16A child under 16 applies in person with Form DS-11. Bring the child. Both parents or guardians go, or you bring the consent or custody documents on this page. You cannot renew a child's passport, and the passport is valid for 5 years.
