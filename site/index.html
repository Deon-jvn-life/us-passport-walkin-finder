Where to apply for a U.S. passport in South Florida

59 passport acceptance facilities in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties, from the State Department's official locator data on 26 October 2026 [source], with acceptance hours and whether those hours are walk-in or appointment.

Call or check online before you go. The locator says to check with the facility whether you need an appointment [source]. USPS locations direct you to usps.com/scheduler [source]. Hours below are as published in the official locator data on the date shown.

By county

  • Miami-Dade County (30 facilities, 5 with some walk-in hours)
  • Broward County (14 facilities, 4 with some walk-in hours)
  • Palm Beach County (15 facilities, 4 with some walk-in hours)

Who needs to apply in person

  • First-time adult applicants, and adults whose passport was lost, stolen or damaged, issued over 15 years ago, or issued when they were under 16, must apply in person with Form DS-11 [source].
  • All children under 16 must apply in person, usually with both parents or guardians [source]. What to bring for a child under 16.
  • Eligible adults can renew by mail (DS-82) or online, and postal employees should not charge the $35 facility fee for a DS-82 renewal [source].

Miami Passport Agency (urgent travel only)

Omni Center, 1501 Biscayne Blvd, Suite 400, Miami, FL 33132. Monday to Friday 7:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., closed weekends and federal holidays. By appointment only. Miami is in a State Department pilot with the Detroit and San Juan passport agencies: you can make an appointment for urgent international travel within 21 calendar days, or if you need a foreign visa within 28 calendar days. Appointments: 877-487-2778 [source]. This is not an acceptance facility; routine applications go to the places listed on this site.
