Passport walk-in vs appointment in South Florida

In short. On 26 October 2026, 13 of 59 South Florida facilities have at least some walk-in hours. The rest are appointment-flagged for all listed hours. 14 have a conflict between the locator's walk-in flag and the comment text or the operator's own page.

The official locator marks each block of acceptance hours. Its "Walk-in hours" filter counts only hours not flagged "By Appointment" [source]. On 26 October 2026: 13 of 59 South Florida facilities have at least some walk-in hours; the rest are appointment-flagged for all listed hours. Every USPS location in the data shows "To schedule a passport appointment or view additional passport acceptance times, please visit www.usps.com/scheduler." [source]

14 facilities have a conflict between the locator's walk-in flag and the comment text or the operator's own page. They are marked Conflict below. Trust the operator and call.

Miami-Dade Public Library System passport services

North Dade Regional, West Kendall Regional and Naranja Branch libraries accept passport applications by appointment only; one appointment per applicant. Photo service is currently not available. The library's own page lists North Dade Regional Monday hours as 4:30 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. [source]

Miami Passport Agency (urgent travel only)

Omni Center, 1501 Biscayne Blvd, Suite 400, Miami, FL 33132. Get directions to the Miami Passport Agency, opens in a new tab

Monday to Friday 7:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., closed weekends and federal holidays. By appointment only. Miami is in a State Department pilot with the Detroit and San Juan passport agencies: you can make an appointment for urgent international travel within 21 calendar days, or if you need a foreign visa within 28 calendar days. Appointments: 877-487-2778 [source]. This is not an acceptance facility; routine applications go to the places listed on this site.
